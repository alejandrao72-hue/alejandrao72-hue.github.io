The Challenge

Data-heavy product,
overwhelmed users

Messaging Insights serves engineers and operations teams who live inside the product daily — monitoring deliverability rates, hunting for error spikes, and troubleshooting why campaigns underperformed. The existing experience had the right data but made it hard to find, filter, and act on.

My job was to redesign the product to reduce cognitive load, improve how users interact with charts and filters, and ship a chart pattern the entire Twilio design org could reuse.

📊

6 dashboards

Overview, Delivery & Error, Responses, OTP, Scheduled, and Link Shortening — each rebuilt with consistent patterns.

🔍

Complex filtering

Users filter by date range, sender, error code, country, and channel simultaneously. Filters needed a full rethink.

⚡

High-stakes monitoring

A missed error spike can mean thousands of undelivered messages. The interface needed to surface anomalies faster.

Health Score · Overview

A score that
tells you what to do

Redesigned the Health Score overview to surface the 5 subscores — Sent Rate, Compliance, Fraud, Latency, and Engagement — in a scannable, prioritized format. Added product recommendations directly in context, so users know exactly which Twilio features can improve their score without leaving the page.

Delivery & Error · Charts

Charts you can
actually interact with

The original charts were static. I redesigned the interaction model so users can click into any data point, drill down by error code or country, and get a contextualized explanation — all without leaving the dashboard. This became the chart pattern adopted by the broader Twilio design team.

Intelligent Alerts · Detail view

From alert to
resolution in one screen

Redesigned the alert detail page to show not just what happened, but why — with error breakdowns, possible causes, and actionable next steps. Users no longer need to cross-reference multiple tabs to diagnose a spike.

AI Assistant · Navigation

Natural language
troubleshooting

Designed the entry points and conversation UI for the Intelligent Discovery AI Assistant — integrated directly into the Insights console. Users can ask in plain language what's wrong with their messaging traffic and get back specific, data-driven answers.

My Contribution

Four areas,
one cohesive redesign

01

Filter system redesign

Rebuilt how users apply, combine, and clear filters across all dashboards — reducing the steps needed to isolate a problem from 6 to 2.

02

Chart interaction pattern

Defined a reusable chart component with hover states, click-to-drill-down, and contextual tooltips. Adopted by the Twilio design org as a shared pattern across products.

03

Navigation & information architecture

Restructured the sidebar and dashboard hierarchy so users can orient themselves faster and jump between related views without losing context.

04

Health Score + recommendations

Integrated product recommendations into the Health Score view — connecting the "what's wrong" with "how to fix it" in a single, actionable surface.
