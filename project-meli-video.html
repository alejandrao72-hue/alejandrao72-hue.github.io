The Challenge

A new format, zero
reference in the platform

Mercado Libre wanted to explore live commerce, a format already booming in Asia where creators stream live, showcase products in real time, and viewers can buy directly from the feed. The goal was to design what that experience would look like inside the MeLi app, where no live video product had existed before.

My job was to define the UX from scratch: how does a live stream look? How do products appear alongside the video? How do viewers interact, ask questions, and buy, without leaving the stream?

📺 Product challenge

No live format in the platform

MeLi had no existing live video product. Every interaction pattern, screen state, and component had to be designed from zero.

🛍️ Product challenge

Video + commerce in one screen

Balancing a live stream with product cards, chat, and purchase actions, without overwhelming users or breaking the shopping intent.

⚡ Design challenge

Real-time interaction design

Products appear, get highlighted, and disappear as the creator talks about them. Designing that dynamism required careful timing and state management.

🔁 Design challenge

Multiple stream states

The experience changes dramatically: stream active, stream just ended, creator never started. Each state needed its own thoughtful design.

My Approach

Explore fast,
define the model

With no prior live commerce reference inside MeLi, I focused on interaction modeling: defining the rules of the experience before visual polish. What components exist? When do they appear? How do they behave? I explored multiple options for each decision and worked iteratively across three MVP versions to move the product toward pilot.

Screen Design · Live View

Four zones,
one screen

The live screen needed to work as a unified experience: creator header, video in 16:9, a dynamic product carousel, and a live chat that scrolls behind the products. Every element hides on tap to give full screen to the video.

Live screen · Initial state: 4 interaction zones with on-tap hide behavior

Component design · Product cards

Cards that
react in real time

Designed three card states: a highlighted card with an "AHORA" pill for the product being actively showcased, a plain highlighted state, and a standard card. As the creator moves to the next product, the highlight transfers and the pill disappears after 2.5 seconds.

The carousel follows a defined logic: the product the creator is showing stays first, then favorited items, then products ranked by live likes.

Interaction · Product Presentation

Products enter
one at a time

Explored how the product shelf animates as the creator introduces items. Option B shows products starting hidden, then entering one by one, highlighted first, then shifting right as the next product is featured. When the fourth product arrives, the carousel scrolls to reveal it while pushing earlier items left.

Product presentation · Option B: 4 stages of product entry and carousel progression

User flow · Purchase during stream

Buy without
leaving the stream

Tapping a product card opens a product detail overlay within the live experience, keeping the stream context visible. The user can select size and color, complete checkout, and return directly to the live feed once the purchase is confirmed.

The post-purchase screen shows delivery estimate, Mercado Puntos earned, and loyalty discounts, reinforcing the platform's value loop.

Screen state · Stream ended

When the stream
ends gracefully

Designed the "Vivo Finalizado" state: what happens when the creator ends the stream. Products shown during the live remain visible and shoppable, the user can follow the creator, and the app surfaces a smart product list ordered by favorites and public engagement during the stream.

From this state, users land back on MeLi home with a recommendation strip surfacing the stream's products in their browsing history.

Key Design Decisions

Every tap
intentional

01

Controls hide on tap

Mute, video title, share, and overlay controls disappear on a single tap, giving the viewer full screen video when they want it, without a permanent UI footprint.

02

Products first, chat behind

Chat is always visible but scrolls behind the product shelf, reachable by scrolling down, but never competing with purchase intent. The blur softens the transition.

03

The "AHORA" pill

A small contextual pill that appears on the currently featured product and auto-dismisses after 2.5 seconds, nudging attention without interrupting the stream experience.
