Overview

Redesigning how
gig workers join Zubale

Zubale is an e-commerce solutions ecosystem operating across 6 countries in Latin America, with 100+ active clients including Walmart, OXXO, and Cencosud. Their gig workforce — "Zubaleros" — are independent workers who fulfill orders in stores.

The challenge: getting workers registered, trained, and to their first task as fast as possible. The previous onboarding was fragmented across WhatsApp, chatbots, web pages, and manual agent workflows — with a conversion rate under 1% and a $200 CAC.

+340 Walmart stores across 32 cities in Mexico
500 new leads per week needing onboarding
$200 CAC before the revamp — unsustainable at scale
The Problem

Fragmented, slow,
and losing users

Business pain

No single source of truth

Manual processes everywhere. User acquisition team had to manage leads across multiple disconnected channels with no unified view.

Business pain

Low conversion — under 1%

CAC of $200 USD and a 5–10 day time to convert made growth unsustainable. Not enough supply to fulfill operations.

User pain

Lost across channels

Users bounced between WhatsApp agents, WhatsApp groups, web pages, the app, chatbots, and SMS — all asking for the same information.

User pain

Days of waiting

Users had to submit the same documents multiple times through different channels, then wait days for an agent to reply.

My Approach

A cross-functional
hackathon to solve it fast

Rather than a traditional design process, we ran a user acquisition hackathon — bringing together Product, Tech, Design, Data, Marketing, and Operations in one room to define, prototype and test a solution in days.

My role: moderate workshops, lead discovery, design wireframes and prototypes, and run user testing in the field.

The hackathon — 7 teams, 1 goal: redesign onboarding end-to-end

Solution · Phase 1

App-first onboarding
with $ incentives

We moved the entire registration process into the Zubale app — a single, guided flow with monetary incentives for completion built right in.

The goal was clear: users earn their first 500 pesos within 12 hours of registering — creating immediate motivation to complete the process.

Solution · Phase 2 & 3

Live support &
follow-up system

We added a WhatsApp chatbot for live support during registration, and built a tracking system to identify where users dropped off in the funnel — so the team could re-engage at exactly the right moment.

User Testing

Testing at store,
in the real world

In-store usability testing, interviews and phone calls with real leads

The Revamped Flow

Standardized,
configurable onboarding

After the initial hackathon results, we continued iterating toward a fully standardized and configurable onboarding — one that could be adapted per country, per operation, and per brand client like Walmart or Chedraui.

Final onboarding flow — registration, document upload, user profiling and operation matching

Internal tool

Onboarding
configuration tool

We also designed an internal tool for the operations team to configure onboarding settings per brand — vehicle types, bonus amounts, required documents, audiovisual content — without engineering support.

This gave operations full control over the experience for each of their 100+ clients.
