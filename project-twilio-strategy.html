Shipped · GA

Health Score &
Messaging Insights

The Health Score gives customers a single number (0–100) reflecting their overall messaging deliverability. Top issues and recommended actions are surfaced directly in context — no manual digging required.

35,000 parent accounts actively use this view every quarter to monitor their messaging performance and spot issues early.

Beta · AI-powered

Intelligent Discovery
AI Assistant

The AI assistant analyzes traffic patterns in Messaging Logs and Insights, surfacing root causes and actionable recommendations in a conversational panel — no CSV exports or manual analysis needed.

Customers can ask questions like "Why are my messages failing?" and get a structured breakdown by error code, phone number, or carrier in seconds.

Proactive detection

Intelligent Alerts —
proactive by design

Customers receive a detailed notification the moment an anomaly is detected — via email, webhook, or Slack — with full context: error code, carrier, threshold, impact level, and a direct link to troubleshoot.

This was the key shift from reactive to proactive: customers no longer had to discover problems themselves.

Shipped · GA

Alert Details —
in-console diagnosis

The in-console alert detail view shows the dynamic threshold model, error description, possible causes, and step-by-step solutions — all in one place. Customers can also provide feedback directly on whether the alert was helpful.
