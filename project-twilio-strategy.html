The Situation

Twilio customers were
flying blind

Twilio's messaging infrastructure powers billions of messages across SMS, email, and WhatsApp. When something breaks — a carrier filtering spike, a sudden drop in deliverability, a fraud wave — every minute without visibility translates into failed OTPs, missed alerts, and lost revenue for their customers.

Before this initiative, troubleshooting was entirely reactive. There was no proactive detection, no guided diagnosis, and no clear path from "something's wrong" to "here's what to do." Users cobbled together answers from disconnected logs, manual CSV exports, and Twilio support tickets.

My work here was not about designing screens — it was about defining what the system should be: which products to build, how they connect, and what job each one does in the user's troubleshooting journey.

Research · What we heard

The same pain,
across every persona

We ran interviews across 6 customer persona types — from on-call engineers to marketing leads to ISV platform owners. The core frustration was consistent: no one could tell what was happening until it was already a problem.

On-call Engineer
"If the AI bot said it seems like all T-Mobile messages are failing for this reason — that would have saved us 20–30 minutes."
Engineering Manager
"Determining where that problem started is a big challenge… Maybe it's AT&T, maybe it was our partner. It's really hard to isolate."
High-volume sender
"We're always every day looking at carrier filtering, opening tickets to understand why it was filtered and if we can appeal it."
ISV Platform Owner
"She must check each market's sub-account individually — complex and time-consuming across their numerous markets."
Strategic Framework

Defining the
troubleshooting journey

The core output of this strategy work was a 7-step observability journey — a shared framework that aligned design, product, and engineering around what users actually need at each stage of monitoring and troubleshooting. This journey became the north star for every product decision that followed.

Each step maps to a specific product or feature. The question was no longer "what should we build?" but "which step of the journey is broken, and what's the minimum intervention that fixes it?"

Messaging Observability Journey — from detection to optimization
01DetectIntelligent Alerts proactively notify on anomalies via ML→ Intelligent Alerts
02MonitorDashboards show delivery, engagement, and fraud trends over time→ Messaging Insights
03InvestigateAI assistant surfaces root causes from logs and traffic data instantly→ AI Assistant
04DiagnoseDrill into error codes, carriers, subaccounts — Health Score gives the full picture→ Health Score
05FixPersonalized recommendations guide resolution with step-by-step actions→ Recommendations
06ValidateReview Insights and Logs to confirm the fix worked and metrics recovered→ Messaging Logs
07OptimizeTraffic optimization, A/B testing, Engagement Suite for long-term performance→ Engagement Suite
