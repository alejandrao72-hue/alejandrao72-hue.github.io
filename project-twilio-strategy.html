Overview

How we mapped the full
observability journey

Twilio customers — from startup founders to enterprise engineering teams — rely on messaging and email as the backbone of their product. When something breaks, every minute counts. But before this initiative, troubleshooting was reactive, manual, and fragmented across disconnected tools.

I partnered with a Staff Product Designer to lead a comprehensive customer journey initiative: mapping how Twilio's users detect, investigate, fix, and optimize their communications across the full observability stack — Logs, Insights, and Intelligence.

35K+ accounts using Messaging Insights every quarter
125K parent accounts visiting Messaging Logs every quarter
−30m reduction in average troubleshooting time post-launch
The Problem

Reactive by default,
blind by design

Through extensive customer research, we uncovered a critical pattern: customers only found out about issues after they happened. No proactive detection, no guided diagnosis, no clear path from alert to resolution.
